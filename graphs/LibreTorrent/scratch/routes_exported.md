## Open evidence boundaries

- Activity launch framework dispatch is cited only for process-side component construction and lifecycle callback (`ActivityThread`/`Activity`); resolution behavior itself is represented by the matching manifest intent filters. Exported activity entry gate is `ENV other_app`.
- AlarmManager delivery uses app-side PendingIntent/AlarmManager construction plus AOSP process-side receiver invocation. No additional AOSP AlarmManager edge was needed for the proposed route, so the alarm link is marked `via` and `ENV time`.
- AndroidX FileProvider, AppCompat menu dispatch, LiveData and Rx dispatch are outside direct framework-source proof or the app's hand-called edges; do not upgrade these to SOURCE-confirmed callback edges without their library evidence.
- JaCoCo was not examined beyond its manifest registration; no coverage files or coverage results were used.
